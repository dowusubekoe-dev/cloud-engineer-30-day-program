# Day 8 interview drill (out loud, under 2 minutes each)

1. AWS IAM vs Entra ID: what is genuinely different? Cover tenant-wide identity, RBAC scope inheritance, and managed identity versus instance roles.
2. Where do you assign a permission, subscription or resource group, and why? Answer with blast radius and inheritance.
3. Veolia anchor: RBAC on customer-data applications for 500+ users. Name who got which role, at what scope, and how you verified it.
